Popular Custom Shower Features

Tile Selections

Choose from an extensive range of tile options to create your perfect aesthetic. Popular choices for Lake Norman homes include large-format porcelain (12x24" or larger for minimal grout lines), classic subway tile in various sizes and colors, natural stone like marble, travertine, or slate, decorative glass tiles for accent walls, and wood-look porcelain for organic warmth.

Glass Enclosures

Frameless glass doors and panels create a seamless, modern look that showcases your tile work. We offer various glass options including clear, frosted, textured, or tinted glass in different thicknesses. Choose from hinged doors, sliding doors, or fully open walk-in designs.

Multiple Shower Heads

Upgrade your shower experience with multiple water sources. Overhead rainfall showerheads provide luxurious full-body coverage, handheld wands offer flexibility, and wall-mounted body jets create spa-like massage experiences. Many Lake Norman clients install dual shower systems for couples.

Built-In Storage

Eliminate clutter with built-in niches or corner shelves designed into your tile work. We create recessed storage that's both functional and beautiful, perfectly sized for your toiletries.

Bench Seating

Add comfort and safety with built-in shower benches. Tiled to match or complement your shower walls, benches provide a place to sit, shave, or simply relax under the warm water.

Decorative Accents

Personalize your shower with decorative tile bands, mosaic patterns, herringbone or chevron layouts, mixed materials and textures, or metallic accent tiles that catch the light.

The Custom Shower Installation Process

Our comprehensive process ensures exceptional results:

  1. Design Consultation: Visit your Lake Norman home to assess space, discuss your vision, and take measurements.
  2. Material Selection: Choose tiles, fixtures, glass, and accessories from our curated selections.
  3. Custom Planning: Create detailed drawings showing layout, tile patterns, niche placement, and fixture locations.
  4. Demolition (if needed): Carefully remove existing shower or tub with protection for your home.
  5. Waterproofing: Apply professional-grade waterproof membrane systems to prevent moisture damage.
  6. Tile Installation: Expert setting of tiles with proper spacing, alignment, and pattern execution.
  7. Grouting & Sealing: Professional grouting and sealing to protect your investment.
  8. Fixture & Glass Installation: Install shower heads, controls, and frameless glass enclosure.
  9. Final Details: Complete caulking, cleanup, and quality inspection.

Proper Waterproofing is Critical

The most important aspect of custom shower installation isn't visible—it's the waterproofing. We use industry-leading waterproof membranes, proper sloping for drainage, sealed corners and transitions, and tested methods that protect your Lake Norman home from water damage. This behind-the-scenes work ensures your beautiful custom shower performs flawlessly for decades.
