Essential Master Bathroom Features

Double Vanity

The hallmark of master bathrooms. Dual sinks eliminate morning congestion and provide personal space for each person. We design custom vanities with ample counter space, dedicated storage for each user, individual mirrors and lighting, electrical outlets within drawers, and coordinated hardware and finishes.

Luxurious Shower

Upgrade from basic shower-tub combos to spacious walk-in or steam showers. Popular features include frameless glass enclosures, multiple shower heads (rainfall and handheld), body spray jets, built-in seating or benches, recessed storage niches, and custom tile work with accent details.

Soaking Tub

Freestanding tubs create stunning focal points perfect for relaxation. Options include sculptural acrylic designs, classic clawfoot styles, modern rectangular forms, or luxurious air-jet systems. Position near windows for views or create a dedicated bathing alcove.

Ample Storage

Master bathrooms require more storage than other baths. We incorporate custom cabinetry with soft-close drawers, linen closets or built-in shelving, medicine cabinets, towel warmers, and dedicated spaces for toiletries and grooming products.

Superior Lighting

Layer lighting for functionality and ambiance. Include task lighting at vanities (sconces flanking mirrors), ambient overhead lighting, accent lighting for architectural features, dimmer controls for adjustable mood, and natural light through windows or skylights.

Heated Floors

Radiant floor heating adds luxury and comfort. Step onto warm tile floors year-round, enjoy faster drying, and reduce bathroom humidity. Programmable thermostats provide energy efficiency and customized comfort.

Popular Master Bathroom Layouts for Matthews Homes

Split Layout

Separate toilet room provides privacy while others use vanity or shower. Ideal for couples who need to share the space during busy mornings.

Linear Design

Elements arranged in a line along one or two walls. Works well in rectangular spaces and creates efficient workflow.

Open Concept

Freestanding tub as focal point with minimal walls creating spa-like openness. Best for larger Matthews master suites.

His-and-Hers Zones

Mirrored vanities with dedicated grooming areas for each person, meeting in centralized wet area with shower and tub.
