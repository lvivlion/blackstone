Popular Backsplash Materials for Ballantyne Homes

Ceramic and Porcelain Tile

The most popular choice for Ballantyne kitchens, ceramic and porcelain tiles offer durability, versatility, and affordability. Available in countless colors, patterns, and finishes, these tiles can achieve any look from traditional to contemporary.

Glass Tile

Perfect for modern Ballantyne homes, glass tiles reflect light beautifully, making kitchens appear brighter and more spacious. They're available in stunning colors and create a sleek, contemporary aesthetic.

Natural Stone

For luxury Ballantyne estates, natural stone backsplashes like marble, travertine, or slate add timeless elegance and unique character. Each stone piece is one-of-a-kind, creating a truly custom look.

Subway Tile

A classic choice that never goes out of style, subway tile backsplashes complement both traditional and modern Ballantyne kitchens. Available in various colors and can be installed in different patterns for visual interest.
