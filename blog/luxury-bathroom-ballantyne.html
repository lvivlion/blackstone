Signature Features of Ballantyne Luxury Bathrooms

Freestanding Soaking Tubs

Create a stunning focal point with a sculptural freestanding tub. Choose from modern acrylic designs, cast iron classics, or luxurious stone tubs. Position it strategically for maximum visual impact, perhaps near a window with views or as a centerpiece flanked by elegant fixtures.

Walk-In Shower Suites

Expansive walk-in showers featuring frameless glass enclosures, multiple shower heads (rainfall, handheld, body jets), built-in seating, custom tile work, and integrated storage niches. Many Ballantyne clients opt for dual showerheads or even separate his-and-hers shower zones.

Premium Natural Stone

Marble, quartzite, and granite bring timeless elegance and unique character. Consider Calacatta marble for dramatic veining, honed travertine for organic warmth, or luxurious onyx with backlit installations for dramatic effect.

Custom Double Vanities

His-and-hers vanities with premium countertops (quartz, marble, granite), undermount sinks or vessel bowls, custom cabinetry with soft-close drawers, and integrated lighting. Include pullout organizers, electrical outlets in drawers, and dedicated storage for each person's needs.

Designer Fixtures & Hardware

High-end faucets, showerheads, and hardware from premium manufacturers like Kohler, Brizo, or Grohe. Finishes such as polished nickel, matte black, brushed gold, or mixed metals create sophisticated visual interest.

Radiant Floor Heating

Step onto warm tile floors year-round with in-floor heating systems. This luxury amenity adds comfort while also helping to dry floors faster and reduce humidity.
