What is a Steam Shower?

A steam shower is an enclosed shower space with a steam generator that produces warm, humid air creating a steam room experience in your own bathroom. Unlike traditional showers that just clean, steam showers provide therapeutic benefits through moist heat therapy. Modern steam showers combine the functionality of a regular shower with the luxury and health benefits of a steam room.

Steam Shower Components & Features

Steam Generator

The heart of your steam shower system. Generators are sized based on your shower's cubic footage and materials. We install commercial-grade generators from leading manufacturers, typically mounted nearby in a closet, vanity, or basement. Digital controls allow you to set temperature, duration, and steam intensity from inside the shower.

Waterproof Enclosure

Steam showers require completely sealed enclosures to contain steam and humidity. We create watertight installations using proper waterproofing membranes, sealed tile work, vapor barriers, and ceiling systems designed for steam environments. Glass doors must extend to the ceiling to trap steam effectively.

Sloped Ceiling

Properly designed steam shower ceilings are sloped to prevent condensation from dripping on users. We install insulated, waterproof ceilings at a 15-20 degree angle using approved materials that withstand constant humidity exposure.

Built-In Seating

Comfortable seating is essential for relaxing during steam sessions. We build custom tiled benches at ergonomic heights, often with heated seating options for added comfort.

Control Panel

User-friendly digital or touchscreen controls inside your shower allow you to activate steam, adjust temperature and duration, control lighting, and even play music through integrated speakers.

Aromatherapy System

Many steam generators include aromatherapy capabilities, allowing you to add essential oils to your steam for enhanced relaxation and therapeutic benefits like eucalyptus for respiratory relief or lavender for relaxation.

Design Considerations for Huntersville Steam Showers

Size Requirements

Steam showers work best in enclosed spaces of 3x4 feet minimum up to 8x10 feet maximum. Smaller spaces heat faster and use less energy, while larger spaces provide more room to relax.

Material Selection

Choose steam-appropriate materials including porcelain or ceramic tile (stone requires sealing), glass mosaic tiles, solid surface materials, and avoid materials that warp or deteriorate in high humidity.

Ventilation

Proper ventilation prevents moisture problems in your Huntersville bathroom. We install high-quality exhaust fans and ensure adequate air exchange while maintaining steam shower enclosure integrity.

Electrical Requirements

Steam generators require dedicated electrical circuits—typically 220-240V depending on unit size. We coordinate with licensed electricians to ensure safe, code-compliant installations.

Steam Shower vs. Sauna: Understanding the Difference

Steam showers use moist heat (100-115°F with 100% humidity), while saunas use dry heat (150-195°F with low humidity). Steam showers offer milder temperatures that many find more comfortable for longer sessions, better respiratory benefits from moist air, integration with your existing shower space, and typically lower installation costs. Both provide excellent health benefits—the choice depends on personal preference and available space.

Maintenance & Care

Steam showers require minimal maintenance. After each use, squeegee glass doors and wipe down seating. Run the exhaust fan after steaming to remove excess moisture. Monthly, wipe down tiles and clean steam head openings. Annually, drain and descale the generator (especially important in areas with hard water) and check all seals and gaskets.
