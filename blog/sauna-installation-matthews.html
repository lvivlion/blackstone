Health & Wellness Benefits of Regular Sauna Use

Cardiovascular Health

Regular sauna use improves circulation, lowers blood pressure, and supports heart health with effects similar to moderate exercise.

Stress Relief

The warmth and quiet of a sauna promote deep relaxation, reduce cortisol levels, and help melt away daily stress and tension.

Muscle Recovery

Heat therapy accelerates muscle recovery after workouts, reduces soreness, and soothes aches and pains from physical activity or daily strain.

Immune Support

Regular sauna sessions may boost immune function by increasing white blood cell production and helping the body fight off illness.

Skin Health

Deep sweating cleanses pores, improves skin tone and texture, and promotes a healthy, radiant complexion through increased circulation.

Better Sleep

Evening sauna sessions promote deeper, more restful sleep by relaxing the body and establishing healthy pre-sleep routines.

Sauna Types for Matthews Homes

Traditional Finnish Saunas

Classic dry heat saunas using electric or wood-burning heaters create temperatures between 150-195°F with low humidity. The authentic sauna experience can include water poured over heated rocks for brief bursts of steam. Perfect for purists who love traditional heat and the ritual of sauna bathing.

Infrared Saunas

Modern infrared technology heats your body directly rather than the air, operating at lower temperatures (120-140°F) while providing similar benefits. Infrared saunas are energy-efficient, heat up quickly, and many people find them more comfortable for longer sessions. Available in near, mid, and far-infrared spectrum options.

Steam Rooms

For those who prefer moist heat, we can install steam rooms that create a humid, tropical environment. Steam therapy offers unique respiratory and skin benefits with 100% humidity at moderate temperatures.

Combination Units

Some installations combine traditional and infrared elements, giving you the flexibility to choose your preferred heat therapy each session.

Sauna Maintenance & Care

Properly maintained saunas last decades with minimal care:

  • Wipe down benches after each use to remove sweat
  • Allow sauna to fully dry between uses with door open
  • Vacuum periodically to remove dust and debris
  • Check heater elements and controls annually
  • Avoid harsh chemicals; use mild cleaners only when needed
